Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Sleep Sounds Hub ("the app") is a personal desktop tool that creates ambience videos and uploads them to the YouTube channel of the person who runs it. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

  • youtube.upload – to upload videos and set their thumbnails on your channel.
  • youtube.readonly – to read your channel's name so the app can show which channel it uploads to.

The app does not read your email, contacts, comments, watch history, subscribers, analytics or any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

  • The OAuth tokens Google issues are stored only in a file on your own computer and are used only to upload videos you have approved.
  • Video titles, descriptions, schedules and generated media are stored only on your computer.
  • The app has no server. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The only network requests are to Google/YouTube (to sign in and upload) and to Freesound.org (to download public-domain sound recordings).
  • No data is sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder on your computer deletes all of its stored data, including the tokens.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/jonogam/sleep-sounds-hub/issues.
