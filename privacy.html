Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Sleep Sounds Hub ("the app") is a personal desktop tool that creates ambience videos and uploads them to the YouTube channel of the person who runs it. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

  • youtube.upload – to upload videos and set their thumbnails on your channel.
  • youtube.readonly – to read your channel's name, and the public counters (views, likes, comment count) and privacy status of the videos the app uploaded.
  • yt-analytics.readonly – to read your own channel's YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers gained and lost, likes, traffic sources and the search terms viewers used) for a weekly performance report shown only to you.
  • youtube.force-ssl – to read the comments viewers leave on your channel's videos, to post replies to them that you have approved, to hold likely spam for your review and publish or remove it at your request, to keep series playlists of your own videos, and to add a "watch next" link to your own videos' descriptions.

The app does not read your email, contacts, watch history, the identities of your subscribers, or any other Google data, and it never accesses any other channel. Comments are read only on your own channel's videos, and the app never posts a reply you haven't approved.

How the data is used and stored

  • The OAuth tokens Google issues are stored only in a file on your own computer and are used only to upload videos you have approved and to read the statistics described above.
  • Video titles, descriptions, schedules and generated media are stored only on your computer.
  • Statistics and analytics from your channel are stored only on your computer (a local database and weekly report files), so the weekly report can compare performance over time. They are refreshed every day the app runs while it is still authorised; if access is revoked, the app stops collecting them.
  • Viewer comments (the commenter's display name and comment text) are stored only on your computer, so the app can remember which comments it has already handled.
  • The app has no server and sends nothing to the developer. Its network requests go to:
    • Google/YouTube – to sign in, upload, read your statistics and comments, and post approved replies;
    • Freesound.org – to download public-domain sound recordings;
    • Anthropic (the Claude API) – your channel statistics, to write your spoken daily briefing, and the text of a viewer comment with the video's title, to draft a reply for you to approve. Anthropic processes this under its commercial terms;
    • Telegram – the briefings, drafted replies and held comments are sent as messages to your own Telegram account, which is where you approve or reject them.
  • The app uses no cookies, tracking or advertising.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone beyond the services listed above, and the app itself never uses your data to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time on Google's security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

All data the app stores lives in its own folder on your computer. To delete it, delete the app's data and secrets folders (or the whole app folder); this removes the tokens, statistics and reports immediately. If you revoke access, delete these folders within 30 days. Deleting the app's local copy does not delete or change anything on YouTube; videos already uploaded stay on your channel until you remove them in YouTube Studio.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
