Sleep Sounds Hub

Sleep Sounds Hub is a personal tool that produces long-form rain and nature ambience videos for sleep and relaxation and publishes them to its owner's YouTube channel.

It runs on the owner's own computer. It mixes public-domain (CC0) field recordings into multi-hour soundscapes, pairs them with original illustrations, and uses the YouTube Data API to upload the finished videos on a schedule the owner approves in advance.

The app only acts on the YouTube account of the person who signs in to it: it uploads videos and thumbnails, and reads that channel's name, its uploaded videos' statistics and its YouTube Analytics for a private weekly report. It uses YouTube API Services; see the privacy policy for details.
