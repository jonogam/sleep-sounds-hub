Sleep Sounds Hub

Sleep Sounds Hub is a personal tool that produces long-form rain and nature ambience videos for sleep and relaxation and publishes them to its owner's YouTube channel.

It runs on the owner's own computer. It mixes public-domain (CC0) field recordings into multi-hour soundscapes, pairs them with original illustrations, and uses the YouTube Data API to upload the finished videos on a schedule the owner approves in advance.

The app only acts on the YouTube account of the person who signs in to it: it uploads videos and thumbnails, and reads that channel's name, its uploaded videos' statistics and its YouTube Analytics for a private weekly report. It also helps the owner look after their audience: it reads comments on the channel's own videos, drafts replies that the owner approves before anything is posted, holds likely spam for the owner's review, and keeps series playlists and "watch next" links on the owner's own videos. It uses YouTube API Services; see the privacy policy for details.
